About

I am a CONICET postdoctoral fellow at the Research Institute for Signals, Systems and Computational Intelligence, sinc(i) (UNL–CONICET), and an Adjunct Professor at the Facultad de Ingeniería Química, Universidad Nacional del Litoral. My postdoctoral research studies algorithmic fairness through the lens of continual learning.

More broadly, I work on machine learning for medical image analysis, with a focus on models that stay reliable when the data changes: generalization across domains, fairness across demographic groups, and anatomically plausible segmentation and registration.

I received my PhD in Engineering from UNL in 2024, advised by Enzo Ferrante and Diego H. Milone, with a thesis on domain-adaptive and constrained deep learning for realistic medical image registration. Before that, I graduated in Informatics Engineering (UNL, 2019).
